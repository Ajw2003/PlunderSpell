# Audio coverage: which placeholders the indexed libraries already cover

Generated by `python Tools/AudioForge/audioforge.py coverage`; do not edit by hand. Files:
`coverage.csv` (one row per placeholder sound), `calibration.csv` (the check the threshold came from).

Libraries indexed: kenney, 389 files.
**27 of 149 placeholder sounds are covered** (best candidate scores at least **0.45**). Guard voices and music are not counted.

Each placeholder is searched with its AI prompt, or its brief when it has none (prefixed "the sound of"), under its category's length rule. "Covered" only means a candidate worth putting on the review page: listen before keeping one.

## How the threshold was chosen

The 102 sounds that are already library sounds (final `L`, at least one Kenney layer) were searched with their own briefs. Their own file came back in the top 5 for 14 of them (top 10: 20, top 20: 27); the other 88 did not, so a bare score is a weak judge here. The briefs are loose ("a heavy curtain drawn") and the earlier picks were stand-ins chosen by hand, so a miss is not always a wrong answer. The rule used: the highest score on a 0.025 grid that still keeps at least half of the 14 hits, which is **0.45**. It is recomputed on every run.

| top-1 score at least | rows | own file in top 5 | precision | share of hits kept |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.20 | 102 | 14 | 14% | 100% |
| 0.30 | 97 | 13 | 13% | 93% |
| 0.35 | 92 | 12 | 13% | 86% |
| 0.40 | 71 | 10 | 14% | 71% |
| 0.45 | 42 | 8 | 19% | 57% |
| 0.50 | 18 | 5 | 28% | 36% |
| 0.55 | 5 | 2 | 40% | 14% |

How well the top-1 score tells a hit from a miss (AUC, 0.5 is a coin flip): **0.57**. That is close to a coin flip, so **the covered count is an upper bound, not a measurement**. 16 of the 27 covered sounds were searched with a long AI prompt, and long prompts score higher against almost any file (`sfx_spell_ignis_misfire` reaches 0.596 with `card-shove-3.ogg`). The review page is where a candidate is really judged.
