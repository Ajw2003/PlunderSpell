Plunderspell · UI redesign · mockup, 2026-09-28

The UI, brought into the lair at 2 am

Every screen and the raid HUD, redrawn in the pitch bible's own identity: pigment colours with one meaning each, Eczar, Spectral and Overpass Mono, hairline rules and square corners. Each frame below is drawn at the game's 1920×1080 canvas size and scaled to fit, so its measurements carry straight into Unity.

Plan: docs/plans/ui-redesign.md. Source of the identity: docs/generated/design-system/.
