# The hook: Ayden's answers (2026-10-25)

Answers given on the comparison page and the hook options page (`hook-options.html`, four rounds).
They are area 1 direction for the core loops redesign and were sent to that thread as they came in.

- **Loop line:** "Shout spells, rob castles, appease the Ophan that owns you." Naming the Ophan reverses
  the earlier "hint, don't name": a quick search explains the name, "angel" read too positive to be scary,
  and "that owns you" carries the fear and servitude, so the name can keep its mystery.
- **The whole loop, in Ayden's words:** plunder, cast spells, escape alive, sell, upgrade, and meet the
  Ophan's demands until you can grow strong enough to defeat it. Like Lethal Company, but the quota is a
  specific artifact, progression persists, and there is a long goal shared with friends.
- **First spell for a new save:** Frango.
- **Raid length:** every raid is 5 minutes at most (the 15 to 25 hour campaign estimate needs redoing).
- **First raid:** starts in the Lair; goes to High Medieval by default, which you can override; the
  Ophan's first demand is visible before you leave and due at the end of that raid; the Ophan hunts in
  the last 60 seconds. The demanded piece is deep in the vault and you are told so: a real challenge you
  can fail, neither too easy nor impossible, and never an item that cannot be found in that Age.
- **The castle hears ordinary talk** by default, with an off switch.
- **Worth in a raid:** losses shown in the world; numbers only in the Lair and Market.
- **Dead wizards:** a ghost that whispers, and the castle hears it.
- **Playtest:** a gate after the first build phase, before the next.
