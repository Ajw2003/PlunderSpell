Stage 1 of the core-loops work. Raw data, gathered 2026-10-25 from guide sites (`sources.md`).
Each row is: how it finds you, what it does, and what beats it. Where a source left a cell blank,
it says "not given".

## Phasmophobia (the voice game)

- During a hunt the ghost hears players talking and moves toward the voice.
- Saying the ghost's name or certain phrases provokes activity (general knowledge; the Wikipedia
  page names the Ouija board and Monkey Paw as spoken-word items).

## PEAK

- The Scoutmaster appears if one player climbs about 200 m above the rest of the group: a rule that
  punishes leaving your team.
