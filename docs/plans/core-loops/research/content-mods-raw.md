# Raw research 7: the most popular content mods, and what makes them work

Added 2026-10-25 at the owner's request: the most popular mods for Lethal Company, R.E.P.O. and
Minecraft, leaving out performance, library and quality-of-life mods, and what PlunderSpell can
learn from them. Download counts are Thunderstore's and CurseForge's own, read 2026-10-25, rounded
as the sites show them.

## How much of the top is content

| Site | Listing read | Content | Libraries, tools, QoL, performance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thunderstore, Lethal Company | top 60 by downloads | 22 | 38 |
| Thunderstore, R.E.P.O. | top 40 by downloads | 22 | 18 |
| CurseForge, Minecraft | top 50 by downloads | 27 | 23 |

The single most-downloaded thing on every list is a loader or a library (BepInExPack 34 M and 33 M;
JEI 635 M). Content mods sit on top of shared libraries (LethalLib, REPOLib, GeckoLib).

## Content mods, by what they add

### Voices: the game copies your friends

| Mod | Game | Downloads | What it does |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Skinwalkers (RugbugRedfern) | Lethal Company | 17.7 M (deprecated) | Records clips from players' microphones and plays them back at random times from the enemies you choose. Clips stay on the machine and are wiped when the game closes |
| Mirage (qwbarch) | Lethal Company | 6.4 M | The Masked copies one player's voice, suit and cosmetics, chosen when it spawns; all players hear the same line at the same time. "Does not use voice recognition or AI" |
| Mimic (eth9n) | R.E.P.O. | 4.1 M | Records voices every 30 to 120 s into a pool; each enemy plays random clips from it |
| DeadTTS (flipf17) | R.E.P.O. | 4.2 M | Dead players speak through text-to-speech |

### Monsters

| Mod | Game | Downloads | What it does |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Mimics (x753) | Lethal Company | 14.3 M | A monster that always disguises itself as one particular thing; "several strategies for distinguishing and surviving mimics" are left for players to find. Easy mode and a colour-blind mode |
| Wesleys Enemies | R.E.P.O. | 4.5 M | A few new enemies |
| CoilHeadStare | Lethal Company | 5.6 M | The Coil-Head slowly turns to face you while you watch it |
| Lycanites Mobs | Minecraft | 29.2 M | 100+ mobs tied to biomes (fire creatures in lava, Geonach spawn when ores are mined, Spriggans from tall grass and crops); optional random mob events in waves; bosses summoned at altars with a key; tameable mounts; creatures drop equipment parts forged into weapons; every spawn set in JSON |
| Alex's Mobs | Minecraft | 159.2 M | 89 mobs, each with "a purpose": a unique drop, mechanic or function; rare animals drop items when they do a specific action; an in-game Animal Dictionary documents them |

### Items, upgrades and economy

| Mod | Game | Downloads | What it does |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Lategame Upgrades (malco) | Lethal Company | 10.7 M | About 50 upgrades at $50 to $1,000, many levelled; counters to named monsters (Silver Bullets lets the shotgun kill the Ghost Girl; Beekeeper; Fedora Suit stops Butlers attacking a lone player); Hunter sells monster samples; five team contracts (defuse a bomb, hack a laptop, exterminate a nest, an exorcism at an altar with the right items, rescue an employee) |
| MoreUpgrades | R.E.P.O. | 4.8 M | More upgrade items, highly configurable |
| LethalThings (Evaisa) | Lethal Company | 8.6 M | 11 scrap pieces with behaviour (a plush that may set the house on fire), 6 store tools (rocket launcher, hacking tool, flare gun that attracts enemies), a trap, decor, a sign counting the crew's deaths |
| BuyableShotgun, BuyableShotgunShells | Lethal Company | 4.6 M, 5.1 M | A monster's weapon becomes a store item |
| SillyValuables | R.E.P.O. | 2.4 M | 250+ valuables and weapons, some with custom effects and sounds |
| Unique Potions | R.E.P.O. | 2.8 M | Valuable potions and buyable elixirs |
| Shop Items Spawn in Level | R.E.P.O. | 3.2 M | Upgrades can be found in levels, not only bought |
| Waystones, Sophisticated Backpacks, Iron Chests | Minecraft | 324 M, 249 M, 221 M | Travel, carrying and storage with upgrade tiers |
| Create | Minecraft | 216.6 M | Physical, animated machines built from parts that work together, instead of menus |
