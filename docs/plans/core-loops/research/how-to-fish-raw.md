### The loop, as the store and reviews describe it

- Premise: you crash your boat on a small island and must "learn how to fish" to get home.
- "Fish fish, kill fish, sell fish" (Insert Coins review). You start with no gear and no weapons.
- Casting is physics, not a power bar: a good and a bad cast differ by trajectory.
- Fish are caught, then often killed with guns (shotguns, SMGs, assault rifles; dynamite is also
  mentioned). Some creatures are dangerous.
- Sell for cash; buy better rods, weapons and boat engine tuning.
- Quests and bosses unlock new islands, which hold more dangerous creatures and better gear.
- Every fish species has a rare variant to collect ("drip" variants with sunglasses or neon scales
  sell at a large premium and unlock achievements).
- Trick shots pay extra: a mid-air hit on a flying fish counts as a trick shot, with a 5× cash
  multiplier on that kill.
- Gambling: "Gamble for more money" (store page). A casino offers roulette (green pays 35×, red or
  black 2×) and slot machines; casino rewards include legendary skins.
- Cooking: grilling raises a fish's sell value up to 1.5×; overcooking until black destroys it.

The island, boss, casino, trick-shot and cooking details above come from howtofishgame.com, an
unofficial fan guide. Treat them as unverified.

- Islands (per the fan guide): 1 Starter Haven teaches line tension and the grill; 2 The Swarm has
  leaping piranhas and a bait you collect to advance; 3 The Deep Abyss: trade rare species to a
  hermit for the bait that summons the Pufferfish boss. Its trophy must be carried to an NPC while
  seagulls try to steal it (one player runs, the others shoot the gulls). Named bosses: Pufferfish,
  Shark, a "Terrorizing Bird", and a final boss.

### Why it spread, as the sources say it

- "This isn't a conventional fishing simulator": fishing is the easy first goal, and weapons,
  bosses and physics make it unpredictable (Player.One).
- Emergent multiplayer chaos makes "unscripted moments that work particularly well in streams,
  clips and group chats" (Player.One).
- "Low pricing ... removes friction from multiplayer word of mouth" (Player.One). Understood in
  minutes, with no story setup (Univers Simu).
- Fast patches: 8-player lobbies, private sessions, difficulty settings (Player.One).
- Criticisms (Insert Coins, 7/10): fish-sell-upgrade only holds if each island brings something
  genuinely new; a joke-led game can "burn out in two hours"; weak solo.

## Gamble With Your Friends (TEAM GWYF, 1 May 2026)

- A co-op "casino crawler". Up to 6 players share one bank account and have five minutes to meet a
  quota owed to a loan shark; a loss is a shared debt (Gamereactor, WN Hub).
- 500,000 sold by 5 May, 1 million by 9 May, 2 million by 4 Jun 2026 (WN Hub). Peak about 42,800 at
  launch, averaging 11,000 to 14,000 later. 89% positive of 8,800 reviews. Price £6.69.
- Bundled with How to Fish on Steam. Whether the two studios are related was not confirmed.

## Webfishing (lamedeveloper, 12 Oct 2024)

- Began as a free jam game made in 8 days (2022); Steam release 12 Oct 2024.
- 1 to 12 players per server. Fish (hold to reel, click on prompts), metal detecting, an in-game
  guitar. Sell fish or do quests for cash; buy cosmetics and gear; collections are the core goal;
  rare fish from special bait or events; a buyable "fishing buddy" catches for you.
- 3,000+ reviews at 98% positive in week one; peak 10,000. Spread on TikTok and Twitter through
  avatar-launching clips and guitar "concerts"; Polygon called it the "ultimate yapping game".

## Dredge (Black Salt Games, 30 Mar 2023)

- Fishing with a horror night: by day calm, by night fog and a panic meter that rises if you stay
  out late, which brings rocks, crows that steal catch and sea monsters that chase the boat.
- Mutated "aberrant" fish sell for more; fresher catch sells for more. Cargo is a Tetris-style grid.
- Money and found research parts buy engine, light, line and net upgrades.
- 100,000 sold in 24 hours; 1 million by Oct 2023. Praised for the calm-day, horror-night balance;
  criticised for repetitive fishing.
