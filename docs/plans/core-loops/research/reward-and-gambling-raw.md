# Raw research 4 of 4: intermittent reward, quotas and gambling

Stage 1 of the core-loops work. Raw data, gathered 2026-10-25 (`sources.md`).

## Reward psychology

- **Variable ratio reinforcement.** B. F. Skinner's pigeon experiments (1950s): a reward that comes
  after an unpredictable number of responses produces behaviour that persists after rewards stop.
  (Gamespace article; the article itself cites no study for its claims about games.)
- **Post-reinforcement pauses.** A 2024 paper in the *Quarterly Journal of Experimental
  Psychology* found slot-machine players pause longer after a meaningful win, and reported similar
  pauses with loot boxes. (Cited by the Gamespace article; authors not named there.)
- **Near misses and losses disguised as wins** are said to activate reward pathways in slot play.
  The article asserts this without a citation, so treat it as unverified.

## Price swings and timing bets

- Lethal Company's Company pays 33%, 53%, 77%, 100% across the four days of a cycle: selling early
  is safe but cheap; waiting is dear but risky (Lethal Company Wiki).
- Lethal Company's overtime bonus pays (excess ÷ 5) + 15 per day left, rewarding greed beyond the
  quota (Lethal Company Wiki).

## Rating and platform rules to note

- PEGI's 18+ for "prominent gambling imagery" pulled Balatro from sale in some countries until the
  re-rating (Wikipedia).
- YouTube's March 2025 online-casino policy age-restricted many Balatro videos (Wikipedia). For a
  game that spreads by clips, this is a reach risk.
- No real-money purchases are involved in any shape above; all are in-game currency.
