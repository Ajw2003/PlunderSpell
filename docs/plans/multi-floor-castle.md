# Multi-floor castles (design, 2026-10-03)

Follow-up to #197 ("stairs lead somewhere and doors open"). Status: **layout and stairs approved by the
owner on 2026-10-03; the systems section below is waiting for review.** No code yet.

Concept sheets: `docs/art/castle/concept/multi-floor-layout.png` (plans of the three floors and an
exploded three-quarter view) and `multi-floor-sections.png` (a section through the castle and both stair
cells). Generated by `Tools/ArtBible/castle/multi_floor_sheets.py`. Why the order changed and why the
newel drum widened: `docs/6-decisions/Decisions.md`, 2026-10-03.

## Why

The castle is one storey. Every stair climbs to a gallery and stops (survey:
`docs/generated/stair-survey-2026-10-03/survey.txt`), and no openable door exists in any castle
(`CastleLockdown` logs "locks its doors (0)"), so lockdown and Porta do nothing in a real raid. The owner
wants a castle with real floors, built by stacking the existing room modules ("8 × 8 becomes 4 × 4 × 2"),
with connections between floors and doors where zones meet.

## 1. Layout (approved)

| Level | Height of its floor | Cells | Zones |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, keep | +4.60 (slab on the ward walls' top, 4.30) | inner 3 × 3 | Keep, 9 rooms; two are stair heads |
| 0, ground | +0.30 | 5 × 5 inside a radius-3 curtain wall | outer ring 16: OuterBailey; inner ring 8: InnerWard; centre: the down-stair |
| −1, crypt | −3.00 | inner 3 × 3 | Crypt, 9 rooms; the centre is the stair foot; the final chamber is one of the other 8, by seed |

- Going deeper into the castle means going up; the crypt is down.
- The curtain wall stays one storey around the ground floor; the gatehouse stays east; raids still arrive
  by portal, on the ground level only.
- Courtyards are carved only from the bailey ring, and only where every level stays connected.
- About 34 rooms above ground and 9 in the crypt (today about 44 and 1).
- The keep's and crypt's outward faces are sealed. A stair head opens only toward the keep's centre
  cell; the crypt stair's foot opens only north. That makes each stair one door's width at the zone change.

## 2. Stairs (approved)

- Two up-stairs in the west and east inner-ward cells, climbing ward to keep: 4.30 m, 23 risers.
- One down-stair in the ground centre cell, ward to crypt: 3.30 m, 18 risers.
- Each Age uses its period form, from the art bible:

| Age | Form |
|---|---|
| High Medieval | the art bible's newel stair: a clockwise drum in the NE corner of a lobby room |
| Late Medieval | a newel stair in a corner turret, keeping the turret stair's gun-loop |
| Bronze Age | gypsum flights in a dog-leg round a light well |
| Age of Powder | a dog-leg great stair (no sheet yet) |

- Newel drums are **3.0 m across inside, 4.6 m outside**. Narrower, the guards' walk map cannot climb
  them (`Tools/ArtBible/castle/newel_walkmap_check.py`).
- An up-stair is one **double-height module**. Its ground room is the lobby, and the stair head is its
  own floor at level 1, in the cell above. The down-stair is a ground room with a well in its floor, and
  its foot lies in the crypt centre cell below. Each stair module therefore owns two cells, one above the other.

## 3. Doors

- Wooden doors where zones meet:
  - every archway between an inner-ward room and a bailey room (12, fewer where a courtyard takes a bailey cell);
  - the archway out of each stair head into the keep (2);
  - the archway at the crypt stair's foot (1).
- `CastleDoor` is a `NetworkBehaviour` (its open state is a SyncVar), but room modules are built
  locally on every peer from the seed. So doors cannot live inside room prefabs. Instead:
  - a pure `CastleDoorPlanner` lists (module, side) pairs from the layout, the same on every peer;
  - the server spawns one network door per entry after the castle is built;
  - clients receive them as network objects.
- `CastleLockdown` already locks and bars every `CastleDoor` in the scene, and
  `CastleLockdownNavigation.MarkDoor` already prices them in the walk map, so both start working as soon
  as doors exist.
- Art: a door prefab per archway size: 2.60 m wide by each zone's archway height (InnerWard 2.88,
  Keep 3.31, Crypt 2.16), oak leaves. It must be added to PurrNet's network prefab list
  (`Assets/_Project/Net/NetworkPrefabs.asset`).

## 4. Systems (for review)

The approach: **a level becomes a third grid coordinate.** `PlacedModule` gains an `int Level` field
(0 by default) and keeps its `Vector2Int GridPosition`, so the 73 existing references across 19 files
keep compiling. Only the code that must tell levels apart changes.

### 4.1 Layout data and generation (`Runtime/Castle/`)

| What | Today | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `ProceduralCastleData.PlacedModule` | `GridPosition` (x, y) only | add `Level`; world height comes from `CastleLevels.FloorY(level)` (0, +4.30, −3.30 for the module root) |
| `ProceduralCastleGenerator.PlaceModule` (`:385`) | `new Vector3(cell.x * cellSize, 0f, …)` | use the level's height |
| occupancy (`Dictionary<Vector2Int,int>`) | one storey | key by `Vector3Int(x, y, level)` |
| `BuildInterior`, `ZoneForRing` (`:132`, `:207`) | rings 0-3 → Crypt/Keep/Ward/Bailey | ground: ring 2 bailey, ring 1 ward, centre the down-stair; then `BuildKeepLevel` and `BuildCryptLevel` over the inner 3 × 3; the stair cells are fixed |
| `m_curtainWallRadius` | 4 (serialized) | 3, changed in the scene/prefab that holds the generator |
| `SealOpenArchways` / `IsArchwayConnected` (`:494`, `:524`) | 4-neighbours in 2D | 4-neighbours on the same level, plus the stair rules (head opens toward the keep centre only, foot north only) |
| ordering for determinism | ring, then cell | level, then ring, then cell |
| `CastlePathValidator` | rasterises a 2D grid | replaced by reachability on the nav graph, which is already built during generation: every entrance must reach the final chamber |
| `CastleDressingPlanner`, `CastleEntrancePlanner`, `CastleArrivalPlanner`, `CastleSpawnResolver`, `CastleBoundary` | ground grid | unchanged logic, filtered to level 0 |

### 4.2 The walk map (`Runtime/Castle/Navigation/`, Editor `CastleNavTileBaker`)

- **Module lookup.** `CastleNavGrid.ModuleAtGridCell(x, y)` becomes `(x, y, level)`. `CellAt`,
  `ConsiderColumn` and `NearestWalkableCell` look at every module in that column, at most 3. The 3D
  distance they already use picks the right floor, so a point on the keep floor finds the keep cell,
  not the ward room under it.
- **Stair modules.** A stair module's tile covers its 12 × 12 footprint over both storeys. A newel
  column has at most one tread over it per storey, and tiles already hold 2 layers per column, so the
  tile format does not change. The baker scans stair modules from above their full height (9.5 m),
  not one storey.
- **Joining modules (`CastleNavStitcher`).** Today an archway joins a module to its neighbour in the
  same grid cell. Now it joins the neighbour on the level whose floor matches the archway's floor
  height, within one step. That is how a stair head at +4.60 joins the keep room beside it.
- **Unchanged.** Areas, portals, path search, smoothing and the director's movement are untouched:
  they work on cells and links, not on grid positions.

### 4.3 Placement and the raid

- **Loot, guards, fires, patrols:** loot (`LootPlacementPlanner`) and fire anchors are module-local,
  so they follow the module's new height without change. Guard placement (`GuardPlacementPlanner`) and
  patrols (`GuardPatrolPlanner`) work from the walk map.
- **Safe arrival radius:** `GuardPlacementPlanner`'s safe radius around the arrival uses Chebyshev
  distance on the ground. It applies on level 0 only.
- **Guards per level:** the keep and crypt get guards from their zones' rosters, as today.
- **Raid flow:**
  - arrival, the portal, extraction and `RaidDirector.FacingTarget` stay on the ground level;
  - the extraction zone is unchanged;
  - only the seed is sent over the network, as before: levels are a pure function of it.

### 4.4 Senses, sound and atmosphere

- **Sight:** linecasts already treat a floor slab as a wall, so guards cannot see through floors.
- **Hearing:** noise uses a 3D sphere and counts walls between the source and the listener. A floor
  between them counts as one wall, so a fight upstairs is heard below, muffled. That stays.
- **Hue and cry:** it sends guards to the players' positions, and they path through the stairs in the
  3D walk map.
- **Unreachable players:** `GuardReachability`'s floor check uses the level-aware nearest-cell lookup,
  so a player on the keep floor counts as reachable.
- **Fog:** height fog is tuned for one storey (`AtmosphereLook.FogBaseHeight`). The keep sits higher,
  so the night captures (`Tools/Unity/night_captures.sh`) need re-checking and possibly a retune.
- **Keep ceilings:** keep rooms are open to the sky like every room today; roofs stay their own project.
  The ward rooms under the keep get the keep's floor slab as their ceiling.

### 4.5 Art

- **Per Age:** an up-stair module and a down-stair module, in the form from section 2, drawn as room
  sheets first (`docs/art/rooms/`), then built in Blender (`Tools/AssetPipeline/castle_builders*.py`).
- **Doors:** three oak door prefabs, one per archway height. Built once and used by every Age for now.
- **Existing rooms:** the crypt and keep rooms already exist, 5 each per Age, as do the door plugs for
  sealed faces.
- **Placeholder:** until the stairs are modelled, a stair module can be a plain ramp in a box, so the
  floors can be built and tested first.

### 4.6 Tests

- **`CastleGeneratorTests`** (it pins today's layout):
  - the room count per level and per zone;
  - the stairs in their cells;
  - the stair head and foot rules;
  - determinism of (`GridPosition`, `Level`) for a seed.
- **`CastleArrivalTests`:** radius 3.
- **Navigation tests:** a stacked column finds the right floor; a stair joins its two floors; an
  entrance reaches the final chamber through both stairs.
- **New `CastleDoorPlanner` tests:** the doors' count and positions for a seed.
- **Stair survey (`Tools/Unity/eval/stair_survey.cs`):** extended to check that every stair joins its
  two levels on the real walk map.
- **Co-op check:** a guard follows the host's player up a stair and down into the crypt.

## 5. Build order

Each step is one issue under #197 and works on its own:

1. **Levels in the data, the generator and the walk map, with ramp placeholders for stairs.**
   Castles stack, guards and players can walk every floor, and the validator checks it. This is the
   biggest step.
2. **Doors:** the planner, server spawning, the door prefabs and the network prefab entry. Lockdown and
   Porta start to matter.
3. **Stair modules per Age**, drawn and then modelled, replacing the ramps. The art can be done
   alongside 1 and 2.
4. **Tuning:** fog, guards per level, loot per level, and the co-op checks.

## Open questions

- Should the keep's two stair heads be different rooms (a guard room at the top of each), or ordinary
  keep rooms picked at random? The design assumes ordinary rooms.
- Does the hue and cry reach the crypt, or does the crypt stay quiet unless something happens down there?
  The design assumes it reaches everywhere, as today.
