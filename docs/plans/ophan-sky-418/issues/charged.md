# The Ophan's charged beam, and the rooms it scorches open

Part of #418

Once its target has been behind cover for 3 s, it charges for 3 s and fires a beam that kills in one hit where it lands, then waits 10 s before it can charge again. The room it strikes is scorched for the rest of the raid on every machine: its walls are drawn burnt and the slow beam passes through them, though they stay solid to walk into. A charged hit on a player carrying an artifact takes the artifact instead, as the slow beam does.
