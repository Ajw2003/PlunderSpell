Plunderspell · the between-raids hub

The Lair, as a place you stand in

A small vaulted cellar outside of time. The crew sets out through a lit portal, and everything they carry out of the castle comes home through it: every goblet, chest and blade, still heavy and still breakable. Then they decide, item by item, what to sell at the Buyer's Hatch and what to keep in the Hoard. The coin buys things through the door, at the Mystical Market.

What changed after your review
  • The whole haul comes home through the portal as objects, not just weapons.
  • Nothing is sold automatically. Each item is sold at the Buyer's Hatch or stored in the Hoard or on the rack, or left where it lies.
  • A door to the Mystical Market, with green lantern light spilling into the Lair.
  • The automatic trophy shelf is gone. The Hoard replaces it.
  • Two new decisions: where sale money goes (D5), and where the market sits (D6).

The gap

Today the Lair is a number with no room

Today
  • LairHubManager holds debt, gold and era on an empty object inside the raid scene.
  • Play starts a raid after 0.25 s. Nobody ever stands in the Lair.
  • No scene ever changes. The only scene load reloads the raid on death.
  • Picking an era changes nothing. The castle generator ignores it.
  • Extraction turns the haul into a number and destroys the items. They never reach the Lair.
  • The Mystical Market doesn't exist on any branch. It's designed in GitHub issues #26–#29.
  • In co-op every player keeps their own separate debt.
  • The raid scene is not in the build list.
Proposed
  • A separate LairScene you walk around in first person.
  • The haul comes home as objects; the crew sells or keeps each one.
  • A door to the Mystical Market, and a purse to spend there.
  • The host's scene change carries every player: Lair → Raid → Lair.
  • One debt per crew, kept on the host and shown to everyone.
  • A save file that remembers every item in the Lair, where it lies.
  • Both scenes added to the build.
Functionality · the haul's journey

Everything comes home. Nothing is sold until someone sells it.

Extraction records which items crossed the threshold and what each was worth. Back in the Lair they spill out of the portal, and the crew carries each one to a station. The debt only moves when something is sold.

Functionality · eight stations

Each station is one verb, made into an object

1

The Hearth

spawn · gather · revive
  • Four spawn points around the fire. Everyone lands here on first load and after every raid.
  • You can't die in the Lair: health stops at 1. A misfired Ignis still sets your beard alight.
  • Players carried out of the raid wake at the hearth. Players left behind wake on the straw pallet (see D3).
2

The Portals

choose a century · set out · come home
  • Four arches, one per era. The chosen one glows lapis; locked ones are bricked up. Choose with E, or by saying PORTA at it.
  • Set out when every connected player stands in the chalk ring. A 3-second countdown runs; stepping out cancels it. F6 still forces it for the host.
  • Anything a player holds goes through: a weapon, a potion, a candlestick.
  • The ring is also the arrival spill. Every item carried out of the castle lands here, set down gently. Weapons too, and everything else.
3

The Buyer's Hatch

sell
  • A shuttered hatch beside the market door, with a brass balance on the sill. It's where the market buys.
  • Put an item on the pan and its price is chalked on the slate. Ring the bell (E) to accept, and a gloved hand takes it and slides the coin back. Lift it off to decline.
  • A broken item fetches nothing. Two-person items need two people at the pan.
  • Where the coin goes is decision D5.

The only place an item becomes money. Pays the worth recorded when it came home.

4

The Hoard

keep
  • Shelves, two iron-bound chests and open floor, right beside the spill. Set an item in a slot and it snaps in and is listed in the ledger as kept.
  • Keep things to sell later, to take into a raid, or because the market's "stays bought" wares need a home.
  • Capacity is physical: when the shelves and chests are full, they're full.

A reason to hold stock for profit (sets, moving prices) would be market design, issues #28–#29. This proposal doesn't invent one.

5

The Armoury Rack

keep weapons · choose a blade
  • Hang a weapon from the spill or the market on a free hook to store it. E takes it down and equips it; E again hangs it back.
  • Starts with a small starter kit (see D4). Weapons can be sold at the Hatch like anything else.

Limitation: no raid spawns a weapon to pick up yet (issue #17). When one does, it comes home through the spill with no further Lair work.

6

The Market Door

spend
  • A heavy door in the east wall, left ajar, green lantern light spilling through. Walk through into the Mystical Market.
  • This proposal builds the door, the lane beyond with four empty stall booths (Spellmonger, Smith, Alchemist, Curiosity Dealer), the crew purse, and a place in the save for purchases.
  • It doesn't build the wares, prices, mark-up or "stays bought" rules. Those are issues #26–#29 and plug into this.

Room in the Lair scene or its own scene: decision D6.

7

The Ledger

see the debt · count the house
  • An open book: the debt in red ink, the crew's purse, and one line per past raid (era, seed, appraised worth of the haul, players saved).
  • A second page lists every item in the Lair, kept or unsorted, with its worth.
  • The +50 for setting out is written in the moment the crew commits. Beside it, the benefactor's strongbox, where debt payments go.
8

The Practice Circle

set the words on your tongue
  • A chalk ring with targets: a pot for Frango, a candle for Ignis, a crate for Levo, a straw dummy for Somnus and Tonitrus. They reset after breaking.
  • A readout shows the word heard, the recogniser's confidence, how loud you were, and whether it would have misfired.
  • Also the voice test bench the roadmap asks for in milestone M1.

Real spell system, no alarm. A badly aimed Frango still breaks whatever it hits, Hoard included.

Everything in the Lair is saved where it lies. Quit with a chalice on the hearthstone and it's on the hearthstone next time. Nothing vanishes and nothing is sold unless someone rings the bell.

Implementation · the loop across two scenes

The host changes scene and everyone follows

PurrNet's networked scene loading (NetworkManager.sceneModule.LoadSceneAsync) moves every client when the host moves. The Lair's state lives on an object that survives the switch.

Tests. The manifest holds exactly the unbroken items in the zone, and conjured gold keeps its rolled worth. Extraction leaves the debt and purse alone; only selling moves money, split per D5; a broken item sells for nothing. The save round-trips every item's id, worth, position and slot, and migrates PlayerPrefs once. The ready check departs only with everyone in and survives a disconnect. Lair → Raid → Lair keeps one Lair object, and selling the haul lowers the debt.

This changes tested behaviour on purpose. Two existing tests (RaidLoopTests, FullRaidIntegrationTests) assert that extraction pays down the debt. Under this proposal it doesn't, so those checks move from "after extraction" to "after selling at the Hatch". They're rewritten to the new rule, not deleted or skipped.

Your verdict · four decisions

My recommendations are pre-selected

D1

In co-op, whose debt is it?

Four players in one room reading four different ledgers makes no sense.

D2

Which Ages are open?

Every era currently builds the same medieval castle. A Bronze Age door that opens onto a medieval castle is a lie players will notice.

D3

What does a player left behind cost?

Keeps "carry your friend out" a real choice without permanent death.

D4

What's on the rack at the start?

Starting with all eight weapons makes bringing weapons home from other eras pointless before it starts.

D5

When an item is sold, where does the coin go?

The market's rule is that you spend "what's left after the debt". Taken literally, the market stays shut until the whole debt is gone, which is most of the game. The split keeps both the rule and a reason to visit the market.

D6

Where is the Mystical Market?

As a room behind the door, there's no loading screen and bought things can be carried straight to the Hoard. Issue #27 allows either.
