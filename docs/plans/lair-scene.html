Proposal · awaiting your verdict
Plunderspell · the between-raids hub

The Lair, as a place you stand in

A small vaulted cellar outside of time. The crew spawns at the fire, reads the debt, practises their words, takes a weapon off the rack, and sets out by all standing in one lit portal. Every function is an object you walk up to. None of it is a menu.

Written 2026-09-26 against integration/staging-2026-09-15 @ 2c45fef · full text in docs/plans/lair-scene.md · nothing built yet

The gap

Today the Lair is a number with no room

Today
  • LairHubManager holds debt, gold and era on an empty object inside the raid scene.
  • Play starts a raid after 0.25 s. Nobody ever stands in the Lair.
  • No scene ever changes. The only scene load reloads the raid on death.
  • Picking an era changes nothing. The castle generator ignores it.
  • In co-op every player keeps their own separate debt.
  • The raid scene is not in the build list.
Proposed
  • A separate LairScene you walk around in first person.
  • Six stations, one per verb in the design bible.
  • The host's scene change carries every player: Lair → Raid → Lair.
  • One debt per crew, kept on the host and shown to everyone.
  • A save file that remembers raids, trophies and the weapon rack.
  • Both scenes added to the build.
Visuals · the view from the spawn point

Visuals · floor plan

16 m by 10 m, and the only way out is a portal

A barrel-vaulted stone undercroft, 4.5 m to the crown. Damp brick, standing water in two corners. A stair climbs eleven steps and stops at a wall, because the Lair stands outside of time. That also means no sky, exterior or skybox to build. Hover a station card below to find it here, or tap a number here to jump to its card.

Visuals · light and colour

Three light sources, no fill light

The design bible says nothing is lit by nothing. The Lair is its one safe frame: "one candle, one fire, falling away into black."

The hearth

The key light, low in the south of the room. Matches the moodboard's Lair swatch, #6E5320 falling to #2A2012.

One candle

On the ledger lectern. It lights the debt figure and nothing else.

The lit portal

Ground Lapis thrown upward onto faces. The only cold light in the room, and only on the chosen arch.

Orpiment only on gold: trophies and the strongbox.
Verdigris on anything usable: hooks, lectern, keystones.
Lapis for the voice: the practice ring and the portals.
Madder for the debt in red ink, and the embers.
Vellum for all written text. Never white.

No new pigments. The eleven kit pieces (cellar shell, stair, hearth, lectern, strongbox, rack, shelf, portal arch open and bricked, pallet, dummy, pot) are built by the existing Blender pipeline in Tools/AssetPipeline/ from the existing palette atlas.

Functionality · six stations

Each station is one verb from the design bible

1

The Hearth

spawn · gather · revive
  • Four spawn points around the fire. Everyone lands here on first load and after every raid.
  • You can't die in the Lair: health stops at 1. A misfired Ignis still sets your beard alight.
  • Players carried out of the raid wake at the hearth. Players left behind wake on the straw pallet (see D3).
2

The Ledger

see the debt
  • An open book on a lectern: the debt in red ink, the gold banked, one line per past raid (era, seed, worth, players saved).
  • The +50 for setting out is written in the moment the crew commits, so you see the cost before you go.
  • A debt of zero shows the existing DEBT_CLEARED ending as a closed book.

The maths stays exactly as LairHubManager.ApplyExtractionResult does it today, which is already tested.

3

The Portals

choose a century · set out
  • Four arches, one per era. Open ones have a green keystone; the chosen one glows lapis; locked ones are bricked up.
  • Choose by walking up and pressing E, or by saying PORTA at it.
  • Set out when every connected player stands in the chalk ring. A 3-second countdown runs, and anyone stepping out cancels it.
  • F6 still forces departure for the host, so today's playtest key keeps working.
4

The Armoury Rack

choose a blade
  • One hook per weapon the crew owns. E takes it down and equips it; E again hangs it back.
  • Starts with a small starter kit (see D4). The save file records anything added later.

Limitation: no raid code can bring a weapon home yet. Filling the rack from raids is separate raid-side work and isn't part of this proposal.

5

The Practice Circle

set the words on your tongue
  • A chalk ring with targets: a pot for Frango, a candle for Ignis, a crate for Levo, a straw dummy for Somnus and Tonitrus. They reset after breaking.
  • Stand in it and a readout shows the word heard, the recogniser's confidence, how loud you were, and whether it would have misfired.
  • This is also the voice test bench the roadmap asks for in milestone M1.

Uses the real spell system. There's no alarm in the Lair, so noise costs nothing here.

6

The Trophy Shelf

remember what came back
  • After each raid the most valuable item that came home sits on the shelf, with a label giving the era, date and worth.
  • Twelve at most. The oldest drops off.
  • Costs one item id per raid in the save file. No physics, no effect on the economy.
Implementation · the loop across two scenes

The host changes scene and everyone follows

PurrNet's networked scene loading (NetworkManager.sceneModule.LoadSceneAsync) moves every client when the host moves. The Lair's state lives on an object that survives the switch.

Your verdict · four decisions

My recommendations are pre-selected

D1

In co-op, whose debt is it?

Four players in one room reading four different ledgers makes no sense.

D2

Which Ages are open?

Every era currently builds the same medieval castle. A Bronze Age door that opens onto a medieval castle is a lie players will notice.

D3

What does a player left behind cost?

Keeps "carry your friend out" a real choice without permanent death.

D4

What's on the rack at the start?

Starting with all eight weapons makes bringing weapons home from other eras pointless before it starts.
