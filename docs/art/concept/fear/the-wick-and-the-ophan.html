The Wick and the Ophan

The two horrors as they stand now: the Lantern Warden's true form, and the creature that hunts the company through time. Each is drawn at true scale beside the 1.80 m reference human. Both approved on 2026-10-11, with lapis allowed for the Ophan; next they are modelled.

The two-form guard

The Wick, on fire

3.10 m tall, shoulders 0.88 m across, hunched; it stoops to 2.50 m to pass a 2.59 m arch, light first. The man has become his own lantern, and he is burning. His ribs open into the lantern's bars around the candle in his chest, and fire leaks out between them. His head is a cage of bone with the flame inside and the kettle hat lifted on top as the cap, fire jetting from its vents. Fire runs along his back and shoulders, out of the tears in his coat and along its hem. His face hangs open in four flaps, one eye still looking. The glaive has grown into his arm. His real lantern stays where he dropped it, dark.

ApprovedThe turn: 1.6 s from being seen to the hunt, the first 0.9 s spent turning, the one moment to run. Its counter: it sees only by its own fire, so you are hidden outside the light.
The creature and its quota

The Ophan of Hours, beyond fighting

9.6 m tall with an 11 m wingspan; the castle's tallest ceiling is 5.2 m. From Ezekiel and Isaiah: a wheel within a wheel, rims high and dreadful and full of eyes all round, six wings as the seraphim have (two spread, two crossed beneath, two covering its feet), and lightning going out of it. At its heart, one great eye 2.6 m wide, with a portal for a pupil. The magical twist: it is a clock of the world. Its great rim is engraved with the four Ages' years, it stands a heartbeat early and late at once, and stone it passes through is pulled back to raw quarry rock, then closes behind it. It never uses a door.
