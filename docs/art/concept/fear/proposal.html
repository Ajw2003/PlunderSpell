PlunderSpell · fear pass · #386 · concept stage

Two horrors to choose

The approved fear pass needs two new things drawn before anything is modelled: what the common High Medieval guard turns into, and the creature that replaces the debt. Below are three options for each, drawn on the art bible's sheet at true scale beside the 1.80 m reference human. Each option also suggests the counter its look hints at, since the design says every horror's behaviour should tell you how to survive it.

Part A · the two-form guard

What the Lantern Warden turns into

Every option keeps pieces of the man (the kettle hat, the quilted coat, the woad hose, the lantern's flame) so players know which guard it was. Each one moves the light somewhere new, because the lantern is the thing you see first.

Your callIs the Lantern Warden the guard you mean as "the common High Medieval guard"? It is the most common entry in the roster (weight 12 in two zones). Then: which true form, A, B or C? Mixing is fine, for example "A's head on C's body".
Part B · the creature and its quota

The celestial being that hunts through time

It arrives in the castle as the portal's clock runs low and wants specific artifacts. All three use lapis, the art bible's colour for voice and portals, because it comes through time the way your portal does. The bible says no enemy uses lapis, so that is an exception for you to approve. Their counters are only suggestions here; how it hunts is a separate question in the handoff.

Your callWhich creature, A, B or C? And may it use lapis, breaking the bible's "no enemy uses lapis" rule, or should it get its own colour?
After you choose
  1. The chosen designs get full front and side sheets in the bible's style, with build notes and budgets.
  2. Then each is modelled in Blender by script, with a review sheet beside its concept.
  3. Rig, clips (the turn, the reveal, the catch) and the engine import follow, as the handoff sets out.
